The Game Lobby – Strategy & Rules

To win at MRATM88 Live Casino, you need to understand the games. Here is a deep dive into our most popular offerings.

1. Live Baccarat: The King of Casino

Baccarat is the heartbeat of Asian gambling. It has the lowest “House Edge” (only 1.06% on Banker bets), making it the fairest game in the casino.

  • How to Play: You bet on Player, Banker, or Tie. The hand closest to 9 wins.

  • Strategy (The Roadmaps):

    • Big Road: Look for patterns. If “Banker” has won 4 times in a row (The Dragon), many players ride the trend and bet Banker again.

    • The “Tie” Trap: The Tie bet pays 8:1, but the house edge is over 14%. Professional players never bet the Tie. Stick to Player or Banker.

  • Variations at MRATM88:

    • No Commission Baccarat: You get paid 1:1 on Banker wins (no 5% tax), except when Banker wins on 6 (pays 0.5:1).

    • Speed Baccarat: Cards are dealt face up instantly. Rounds take 12 seconds.

2. Live Roulette: The Devil’s Wheel

Roulette offers the highest flexibility. You can play safe (Red/Black) or play risky (Single Number).

  • European vs. American: Always play European Roulette (Single Zero). The American wheel has a “Double Zero” (00), which doubles the house edge against you. MRATM88 clearly labels these tables.

  • Lightning Roulette: This is the most popular version today. In every round, 1 to 5 “Lucky Numbers” are struck by lightning. If you bet on that number and it wins, you get paid 50x to 500x (instead of the standard 35x).

  • Strategy: The “Martingale” (doubling your bet after a loss) is dangerous. We recommend “Tier Bets”—covering a specific section of the wheel to increase your coverage.

3. Live Sic Bo: The Dice Game

A fast-paced game involving three dice.

  • The Big/Small Strategy: Betting Big (Total 11-17) or Small (Total 4-10) is the safest play, paying 1:1.

  • The Triple Hunt: Betting on a specific “Triple” (e.g., three 4s) pays 150:1. It is a long shot, but hitting it can recover an entire night’s losses.

4. Live Blackjack: Skill Meets Luck

This is the only game where your decision matters.

  • The Rules: Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over (“Busting”).

  • Basic Strategy:

    • Always split Aces and 8s.

    • If Dealer shows a 4, 5, or 6, they are weak. You should “Stand” on 12 or higher to let them bust.
