Understanding Betting Odds: Asian Handicap, 1X2 & More

One of the first hurdles for new sports bettors is understanding how different odds formats work. MrAtm88’s sportsbook primarily uses odds formats that are familiar to Asian bettors, but also supports European formats for those who prefer them. Here is a breakdown of the most important odds types available:

Asian Handicap

Asian Handicap is the dominant odds format in Malaysian and Southeast Asian sports betting, and for excellent reason — it eliminates the draw outcome, giving bettors a 50/50 chance on most matches (adjusted for team quality differences). In an Asian Handicap market, the stronger team is given a handicap (for example, -1.5 goals) and the weaker team receives an advantage (+1.5 goals). You bet on whether the handicapped result favours your chosen team.

For example: if Liverpool (-1.5) is playing against Crystal Palace (+1.5), a bet on Liverpool -1.5 wins if Liverpool wins by 2 or more goals. A bet on Crystal Palace +1.5 wins if Crystal Palace wins, draws, or loses by exactly 1 goal. Asian Handicap with whole number handicaps (e.g., -1) can result in a half-return (push) if the game ends exactly on the handicap line, providing an additional layer of protection against losing your full stake.

1X2 (Match Result)

1X2 is the simplest form of football betting — you bet on whether the home team wins (1), it ends in a draw (X), or the away team wins (2). This format is straightforward and familiar, and is available for most football matches at MrAtm88. The odds on 1X2 markets are typically expressed in decimal format (e.g., 2.50 for a home win), where your return = stake × odds.

Over/Under (Total Goals)

Over/Under betting asks you to predict whether the total number of goals (or points, in basketball) in a match will be above or below a specified line. For example, an Over/Under 2.5 goals market asks you to predict whether there will be 3 or more goals (Over) or 2 or fewer goals (Under). This type of betting is popular because it is not dependent on picking the winning team — even a 0-0 draw produces a valid result for Under bettors.

Both Teams to Score (BTTS)

BTTS markets ask you to predict whether both teams in a match will score at least one goal, regardless of the final result. If you back BTTS Yes and the match ends 2-1, your bet wins. This market has gained significant popularity among football bettors who prefer to focus on attacking potential rather than predicting an exact scoreline.

Sports Betting Strategies for Malaysian Bettors

Successful sports betting over the long term requires discipline, research, and a clear strategic framework. While there is no guarantee of profit in sports betting, the following principles significantly improve your chances of generating positive returns.

Specialise in one or two leagues or sports. Bettors who spread their action across dozens of leagues and sports rarely develop the in-depth knowledge needed to consistently identify value. Instead, focus intensively on one or two competitions you follow closely. Deep knowledge of team form, injury news, tactical trends, and historical head-to-head records gives you an information edge that casual bettors lack.

Bet for value, not just winners. A bet is valuable when the probability of an outcome is higher than the implied probability suggested by the odds. A team priced at 2.50 (40% implied probability) is a value bet if you believe their true winning probability is actually 50% or higher. Consistent value betting is the foundation of every profitable long-term sports betting strategy.

Keep detailed records of every bet. Track your bet selections, stakes, odds, and outcomes in a spreadsheet or betting journal. This discipline reveals patterns in your betting behaviour — which sports you perform best in, which bet types generate profit, and where your losses are concentrated. Without records, you cannot identify areas for improvement.

Use a flat staking plan. Bet the same percentage of your bankroll on every wager rather than varying stakes based on confidence. Professional bettors typically risk 1–3% of their bankroll per bet. Flat staking protects you from catastrophic drawdown periods and ensures that a losing streak, however long, cannot eliminate your bankroll.
